# ADR-004: Repository license

- Identifier: ADR-004
- Date: 2026-08-02
- Status: Approved
- Decision owner: Aitor Nain Mendoza Vallejo

## Context

The public portfolio repository needs explicit reuse terms. The license must permit commercial viewing, reuse and adaptation without introducing a paid dependency or a copyleft obligation that could discourage portfolio evaluation.

## Decision

License the repository under the OSI-approved MIT License with copyright held by Aitor Nain Mendoza Vallejo.

## Consequences

- The source may be used, modified, distributed, sublicensed and sold subject to retaining the license and copyright notice.
- The software is supplied without warranty under the license terms.
- The license does not transfer ownership of trademarks, portfolio content, third-party services or data.
- All included dependencies keep their own licenses and still require dependency review.

## Rejected alternatives

- **No license:** rejected because public source would have unclear reuse rights.
- **GPL-family license:** rejected because reciprocal obligations are unnecessary for this portfolio objective.
- **Proprietary license:** rejected because it reduces inspectability and reuse without validated commercial advantage.

## Reconsideration trigger

Reconsider before accepting third-party contributions with incompatible terms or relicensing a commercial edition.
